# Surface brief: Super Treino Academia, home and unit pages in three layouts

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: prospective student in Teresina, mostly on mobile from Google or Instagram; also the academy owner judging three prototypes. Action: pick the closest unit and open its WhatsApp (pre-filled message), or call or get directions. Proof on hand: 4.7 Google rating (283 reviews), real addresses and phones (to confirm), Itararé plan prices (to confirm), dance class days per unit, provisional AI photos. Unknown facts stay marked, never invented.

Three worlds were approved on the canvas and are pinned: A "Ingresso de festa", C "Noite", D "Contraste". They are inherited, not re-rolled. Special effects serve conversion: each variant owns one signature interaction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gym site whose first act is choosing the unit and tapping WhatsApp, not a stock hero with feature cards. Each layout makes that act its own object: a punched ticket (A), a lit night room (C), a black and white poster that turns to colour (D).

OWN-WORLD: A: royal blue ground, golden yellow tickets with perforation, Big Shoulders Display and Figtree, grain, tilt-on-pointer tickets, confetti burst on WhatsApp taps. C: near-black ink, full-bleed photography, Archivo condensed and Albert Sans, yellow rules, a cursor lantern in the hero and cards, masked word-by-word headlines, magnetic buttons. D: pure black, Teko and Manrope, grayscale photos that gain colour at centre screen or on hover, ring cursor, flat pill buttons.

STORY: Visitor sees three units, a visible price ladder and the class grid, believes this is a welcoming neighbourhood gym with fair prices, and taps WhatsApp for one unit within two taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline "Treine como em casa" at display scale left, unit choice with a WhatsApp action visible without scrolling (A: ticket with three units; C: bar under the hero photo; D: stat row and trial-class chip), Google 4,7 proof beside the headline, primary CTA top right.

FORM: Pinned by the user from the approved canvas, positions A, C, D of the shown hand. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
